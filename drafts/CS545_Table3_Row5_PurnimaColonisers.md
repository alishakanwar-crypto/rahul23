### ROW 5 OF TABLE 3 — NOW VERIFIED: THE PURNIMA COLONISERS (2018 O&M) COUNTER ADMITS THE SCUFFLE AND THE MLC

Source: **CP No. 206/241-242/ND/2018, NCLT New Delhi — Purnima Gupta v. Purnima Colonizers Pvt. Ltd. & Ors.** (oppression & mismanagement petition by Purnima Gupta, wife of Rahul Gupta). The Reply on behalf of Respondent Nos. 1 to 4 was **sworn by Mr. Mohit Gupta (Respondent No. 4, Director of Respondent No. 1)** and, per its own para 4, is "also being adopted and affirmed by respondent No. two and three" — i.e. **Mrs. Veena Gupta (R-2) and Mr. Mukesh Gupta (R-3)**.

Their stand there — verbatim:

> (Reply to paras 9–10) "It is however **a matter of record that a scuffle did take place between family members** when the petitioner's husband (Rahul Gupta) attempted to snatch away the keys of one of the vaults at PP Jewellers Pvt. Ltd. Frustrated at the failed attempt to take control of the jewelry stock, Rahul and his wife (petitioner herein) **left the family home in the month of November 2015**."

> (Reply on the alleged assault) "...the scuffle took place only because the petitioner's husband was attempting to take control of the vault where the jewelry of the family business was kept. **The MLC placed on record clearly reflects that a minor scuffle took place and some scratches were present on physical self of the petitioner's husband.**"

> (Reply to para 27) "...it is the petitioner, who as per her own case, has walked out of the family home as well as the business and has made and supported allegations by her husband of **having been criminally assaulted by their own family members**..."

The same Reply also admits that, prior to November 2015, "all statutory compliances including filing of returns, audited balance sheets and other financial statements... was being looked after and giving effect to by the petitioner's husband" (Rahul Gupta) who also maintained "all books of accounts and other statutory records" and handled "banking operations" — i.e., he was inside the family business until the November 2015 incident.

**Why this destroys the Reply's attack on para 15 of the plaint:** In 2019, before the NCLT, Veena Gupta, Mukesh Gupta and Mohit Gupta — through a reply sworn by Mohit Gupta — ADMITTED on record that (i) a physical scuffle between family members took place at the PP Jewellers vault, (ii) the MLC records injuries ("scratches") on Rahul Gupta's person, and (iii) Rahul Gupta and his wife left the family home in November 2015. Their only quarrel was with characterisation ("minor scuffle", "exaggerated claim"). Defendant Nos. 1–2 cannot now suggest the 06.11.2015 incident/MLC is a fabrication or a contradiction — the incident, the MLC and the November 2015 exit are common ground on their own side's sworn pleadings. Whether it was a "minor scuffle" or a violent assault/ouster is a triable question of fact; either way the family-wide admission of the November 2015 exclusion supports the Plaintiff's case of ouster and continuing exclusion.
