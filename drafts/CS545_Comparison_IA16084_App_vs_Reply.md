## Key patterns visible from the comparison (for the rejoinder)

1. **What they ADMIT (usable):** the four alienations of para 86 (and additionally the November 2025 sale of 2129–2130 Gurudwara Road, Karol Bagh disclosed in their reply to para 96 — a sale AFTER the 13.08.2025 SC judgment); the Plaintiff's physical possession of Rawta (para 95); the Plaintiff's non-signatory status to the 2015/2019 documents (paras 19, 28); the record of the 22.03.2024 statement (para 73(a)); the filing chronology (paras 26, 28–30, 72, 74–75, 77, 81–82, 89).
2. **Their four recurring shields:** (i) "no concluded settlement — only in-principle"; (ii) approbate/reprobate against us on the 2015/2019 documents; (iii) limitation from Nov 2015; (iv) sales were lawful-owner debt-repayment. Each is a triable merits defence, not an answer to corpus preservation — none explains why status quo pendente lite injures a defendant with a bona fide defence.
3. **Their own approbate/reprobate:** while denying any concluded settlement they invoke the FSD/MOU when convenient — Rawta "at the highest only a 50% share" (para 88), the equalisation mechanism (paras 19, 93), the 2019 shares' inter-se relevance (para 20), and the entire ₹698.54 Cr assets-with-liabilities construct (paras 1, 11, 21).
4. **Blank/merged replies:** their para 27 is blank; para 28 of the Reply answers our paras 27 and 28 together — worth noting formally.
5. **New factual allegations needing verification before rejoinder (counsel to verify — do not adopt):** the alleged 08.06.2026 Rawta sale deed/contempt-of-status-quo narrative (their para 75/preliminary section H); the November 2025 sale of Karol Bagh 2129–2130 by D2 (para 96); the forged-partnership-deed allegation (paras 25, 87); the JLF Minutes 18.11.2015 contents (paras 9, 14, 18); the alleged contradiction with Cont.Cas.(C) 1044/2026 (para 15); the "It's My Name" pleading in I.A. 13282/2023 (para 102).
