### COUNTER TO D1–2's PARA-WISE REPLY, PARA 3 — ORDER DATED 19.03.2024 IN CONT.CAS.(C) 1058/2023 (DELHI HIGH COURT)

D1–2's Reply (para-wise reply, para 3) asserts that Defendant No. 36 (PP Jewellers Retail Pvt. Ltd.) holds substantial jewellery stock only because Mr. Pawan Gupta "diverted the business, customers, goodwill and jewellery stock" of M/s P.P. Jewellers Pvt. Ltd.'s Karol Bagh showroom to Defendant No. 36 — i.e., the depletion of the corpus stock is blamed entirely on the PG Group, and the allegations in para 3 of the application are dismissed as "vague" and irrelevant.

This is directly countered by the **order dated 19.03.2024 of the Hon'ble Delhi High Court (Mini Pushkarna, J.) in CM APPL. 17055/2024 in Cont.Cas.(C) 1058/2023 — M/s Purnima Associates Pvt. Ltd. v. Sh. Kamal Kumar Gupta, Director of M/s PP Jewellers Pvt. Ltd.** In that contempt application (disobedience of the DRT restraint order dated 22.03.2017 in O.A. No. 292/2017), the Court recorded:

1. The **DRT order dated 22.03.2017** restrained alienation/encumbrance of the very corpus properties in this suit — 2129–2130 Gurudwara Road, Karol Bagh; C-8/4 and **C-19, Rana Pratap Bagh**; Plot No. 23 adjoining A-332, Defence Colony; 57 Burtala Street, Kolkata; and the "hypothecated stocks and machinery lying at P.P. Tower, H-5, Netaji Subhash Place, Pitam Pura" (order, paras 1–2).

2. It was submitted, with Annexure-3, that despite the restraint, **"the respondents have transferred stocks to a related company i.e. M/s PP Jewellers Retails Private Limited"** for the period 01.04.2017 to 31.03.2018, and that the transferred materials were **not shown in PP Jewellers Pvt. Ltd.'s balance sheet** — "in order to circumvent the restraint order dated 22nd March, 2017" (order, paras 3–5).

3. The Local Commissioner's report (visit of 23.01.2020 to PP Tower, H-5) records that **Mr. Mohit Gupta, son of Mr. Kamal Kumar Gupta**, together with Ms. Anita Kathurie (Manager), was present and assisting at the showroom (order, para 6) — i.e., the stock and showroom were under the KG Group's operational control, not the Plaintiff's or the PG Group's.

4. The stock statement showed **transfer of stock to Durga Trading Co., Shree Ganesh Jewellers, DRS Jewellers Pvt. Ltd., Jaina Jewellers, Anurag Jewellers NAC and New Shakti Abhushan Bhandar**; Shree Ganesh Jewellers was registered on 27.09.2017 (after the restraint order) in the name of Ms. Anita Kathurie, PPJPL's own erstwhile manager (order, paras 7–9).

5. It was submitted that after "siphoning of the stocks and various amounts to other companies", the respondents declared **Rs. 208,64,34,916/- as allowance for Bad and Doubtful debts as on 31.03.2021** (order, para 10).

6. On this material the Court **issued notice** and specifically **DIRECTED that "Mr. Kamal Kumar Gupta and Mr. Pawan Kumar Gupta shall specifically give details of all the transactions that have been made by PP Jewellers Private Limited after the restraint order dated 22nd March, 2017"** (order, paras 11–14).

**The point in rejoinder:** Defendant No. 1 cannot, in reply para 3, attribute the depletion of the PPJPL stock solely to Mr. Pawan Gupta's alleged "diversion" when, before another Bench of this Hon'ble Court, there is judicially-recorded prima facie material that stocks of PPJPL — a corpus company under the KG Group's own control (as the Local Commissioner's report shows) — were transferred to PPJ Retail and to a web of entities including one floated in the name of PPJPL's own manager, in the teeth of a subsisting DRT restraint, with over ₹208 crores then written off as bad and doubtful debts, and that Kamal Gupta himself stands directed to account for all post-22.03.2017 transactions of PPJPL. Far from being "vague" or irrelevant, the dissipation of corpus stock is documented and pending judicial scrutiny — and it is the answering Defendants who must account. This reinforces both the prima facie case and the urgency of preserving the remaining corpus, and supplies yet another instance of the answering Defendants approbating and reprobating (blaming diversion on others while themselves facing a contempt-level accounting for the same stock).

(Counsel note: the order of 19.03.2024 records submissions of the applicant and issues notice — it is not a final finding; frame it as judicially-recorded material and a subsisting direction to account, not as a conclusive finding of siphoning.
