## APPLICATION UNDER SECTION 151 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908 ON BEHALF OF THE PLAINTIFF FOR PLACING ON RECORD SUBSEQUENT EVENTS AND MATERIAL FACTS AND RESTATING THE URGENCY AFRESH

### MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

**A. BACKGROUND**

1. That the Plaintiff company is promoted by the L.R. family. The Plaintiff and its promoter family are entirely distinct from, and strangers to, the family of the Defendants (the PP Jewellers family). The only connection between the two families is matrimonial — Ms. Purnima Gupta, a daughter of the L.R. family, is married into the PP Jewellers family. The Plaintiff is not a party to, and has no concern with, the internal family arrangements of the PP Jewellers family.

2. That the present suit seeks, inter alia, vacant possession of the basement, first, second and third floors of property No. H-5, Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura, New Delhi-110034 ("Suit Property"), owned by the Plaintiff under the registered Perpetual Lease Deed dated 29.12.1998 and Conveyance Deed, and in the wrongful possession of Defendant No. 1.

3. That Defendant No. 1 has filed I.A. No. 22890/2025 under Order VII Rule 11 CPC and a reply to the Plaintiff's application for exemption from pre-institution mediation under Section 12A of the Commercial Courts Act, 2015 (I.A. No. 21389/2025), both resting principally on the plea that there is no urgency and no threat to the Suit Property. The present application places on record subsequent events and material facts which demonstrate that the urgency pleaded in support of the Section 12A exemption is real, continuing and, in fact, aggravated.

**B. URGENCY RESTATED AFRESH — THE SECTION 12A EXEMPTION STANDS REINFORCED BY SUBSEQUENT EVENTS**

4. That the urgency pleaded at the institution of this suit, in support of the exemption from pre-institution mediation (I.A. No. 21389/2025), has not abated; it has aggravated, for the following reasons:

(a) **Allocation of possession of the Suit Property — the principal new urgency:** In the petition under Section 482 CrPC, being CRL.M.C. No. 1037/2026, which concerns this very Suit Property (H-5), the stand of the PP Jewellers family side is that, under the internal family settlement documents of the PP Jewellers family — including the Family Settlement Deed/MOU dated 09.07.2019 — possession of the property held by P.P. Jewellers entities (including Defendant No. 1/PPJ and PP(D)E) stands **allocated to Mr. Kamal Gupta**. Defendant No. 1 thus does not even claim possession of the Suit Property in its own corporate right; possession of the Suit Property is being dealt with and allocated to third parties under the PP Jewellers family's private documents, to which the Plaintiff and the L.R. family — the owner of the Suit Property and its promoters — are complete strangers. The apprehension of the Suit Property being dealt with, and of third-party interests being set up in it, is therefore neither hypothetical nor stale, and constitutes a fresh and continuing urgency.

(b) **The contradiction:** The aforesaid stand is in stark contradiction to what Defendant No. 1 has told this Hon'ble Court in this very suit: in its reply to the Plaintiff's application for exemption under Section 12A (I.A. No. 21389/2025), Defendant No. 1 asserts that it has *no intention of creating any third-party rights* in the Suit Property — while, behind the Plaintiff's back, in CRL.M.C. No. 1037/2026, possession of the same property is asserted to stand allocated to Mr. Kamal Gupta. A pictorial depiction of the contradictory stands is set out below:

![Contradictory stands of Defendant No. 1](file:///home/ubuntu/Downloads/CS921_contradiction.png)

(c) **Contempt proceedings for such dealings:** The PP Jewellers family side is facing contempt proceedings — including Cont.Cas(C) No. 1044/2026 and the contempt proceedings arising out of the undertakings recorded in the order dated 22.03.2024 in ARB.P. No. 1010/2022 — in respect of dealing with/creating rights in properties contrary to undertakings given to the Court. The risk to the Suit Property from such conduct is demonstrated, not speculative.

(d) **Only the ground floor is protected:** The protection in CS(COMM) No. 671/2025 (order dated 09.07.2025) concerns only the ground floor of H-5; the basement and the first to third floors — the subject matter of this suit — enjoy no protection whatsoever. The Defendants' own reliance on that order concedes that judicial protection is what secures the property; the remaining floors require the same.

(e) **No merits reply on possession:** The Defendants' filings in this suit are threshold objections alone (Section 12A; Order VII Rule 11 CPC); none of them discloses any answer on the merits to the Plaintiff's title under the registered Perpetual Lease Deed dated 29.12.1998 and the Conveyance Deed.

5. That the Plaintiff's urgency is to be assessed from the standpoint of the Plaintiff (*Dhanbad Fuels Pvt. Ltd. v. Union of India*, 2025 INSC 696), and the suit as framed contemplates urgent interim relief (*NOVENCO Building & Industry A/S v. Xero Energy Engineering Solutions*, 2025 INSC 1256). The subsequent events set out above independently satisfy that test afresh, and the exemption under Section 12A (I.A. No. 21389/2025) deserves to be allowed.

**C. IMPLEADMENT OF MR. KAMAL GUPTA**

6. That in view of the claimed allocation of possession of the Suit Property to Mr. Kamal Gupta under the aforesaid family settlement documents of the PP Jewellers family, Mr. Kamal Gupta is a necessary and/or proper party for the complete and effectual adjudication of the questions involved in the suit, including for binding him to any decree of possession and any interim orders concerning the Suit Property. The Plaintiff seeks leave to implead him as a defendant under Order I Rule 10(2) CPC, and is filing/shall file a separate application to that effect; alternatively, this Hon'ble Court may direct his impleadment in exercise of its powers under Order I Rule 10(2) CPC.

**D. CONSEQUENCES**

7. That in the circumstances aforesaid, the threshold applications of the Defendants ought not to defer the consideration of the Plaintiff's applications; the exemption under Section 12A deserves to be allowed in view of the aggravated urgency; and interim protection over the Suit Property is warranted.

8. That the present application is bona fide and in the interest of justice.

### PRAYER

It is, therefore, most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may graciously be pleased to:

(a) take on record the subsequent events and material facts set out hereinabove;

(b) allow the Plaintiff's application for exemption from pre-institution mediation under Section 12A of the Commercial Courts Act, 2015 (I.A. No. 21389/2025) in view of the urgency restated hereinabove;

(c) in the interim, restrain the Defendants, their agents, assigns and all persons claiming under them, from parting with possession of, alienating, encumbering or creating any third-party rights in the Suit Property (basement, first, second and third floors of property No. H-5, Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura, New Delhi-110034) pending disposal of the suit;

(d) implead Mr. Kamal Gupta as a defendant to the suit under Order I Rule 10(2) CPC, in view of the claimed allocation of possession of the Suit Property to him under the family settlement documents of the PP Jewellers family;

(e) direct that the Defendants' applications under Section 12A / Order VII Rule 11 CPC shall not defer the consideration of the Plaintiff's interim application;

(f) award costs, including under Sections 35, 35A and 35B CPC; and

(g) pass such other and further order(s) as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the facts and circumstances of the case.
