## PART III: STRATEGY FOR INTERIM RELIEF (WAY FORWARD)

### THE CORE LEGAL POSITION: Mere filing of SLP does NOT stay DHC proceedings

**Key case laws:**

1. **Shiv Shankar Dal Mills v. State of Haryana, (1980) 2 SCC 437:**
   > *"The mere filing of a special leave petition does not operate as a stay of the order or proceedings impugned."*

2. **Asian Resurfacing of Road Agency v. CDA, (2018) 16 SCC 299:**
   SC held proceedings should not be stayed merely because an SLP is pending. The court below retains full jurisdiction unless the SC has specifically granted a stay.

3. **Raman & Raman Ltd. v. State of Madras, AIR 1959 SC 694:**
   > *"Unless the Supreme Court passes a stay order, the filing of a Special Leave Petition does not affect the jurisdiction of the court below."*

**No stay has been granted by the SC.** The SLP has merely been NUMBERED (Diary No. 37707). Until and unless the SC specifically stays the DHC proceedings, the DHC is free to — and indeed MUST — proceed to hear I.A. 16084.
