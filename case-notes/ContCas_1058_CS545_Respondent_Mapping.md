## 3. WHY R-36, R-37, R-38 ARE NECESSARY PARTIES IN CS(OS) 545/2026

### A. The Three Entities (Likely R-36, R-37, R-38):

> **NOTE:** The exact mapping of which entity is R-36, R-37, or R-38 in CS(OS) 545/2026 needs to be confirmed from the plaint's memo of parties. The user should verify.

### B. Legal Justification — Why They Must Be Parties:

#### (i) Necessary Parties Under Order 1 Rule 10 CPC:
These entities are **necessary parties** because effective adjudication of the partition suit requires their presence:
- They hold, manage, or have received PPJ Corpus assets
- Any decree for partition would be incomplete without binding these entities
- **Ramesh Hirachand Kundanmal v. Municipal Corporation of Greater Bombay (1992) 2 SCC 524** — A party without whom no effective decree can be passed is a necessary party

#### (ii) They Are Corporate Vehicles of the PPJ Family:
- **Venkatesh Jewellers** — Though nominally controlled by Rashi Khemka (an outsider from Nagpur), this entity was impleaded in the contempt proceedings because it was used by the Gupta family to deal with PPJ Corpus assets in violation of DRT orders
- **PP Jewellers Retail** — Directly under Pawan Gupta (D-7 in CS(OS) 545). This is the retail arm of the PPJ business — its stock and receivables form part of the PPJ Corpus sought to be partitioned
- **Veekay Exim** — Under Mohit Gupta (Pawan's son). Another family entity that may hold PPJ Corpus assets or receivables

#### (iii) Contempt Proceedings Prove Their Involvement in PPJ Corpus:
The fact that these very entities/persons were **impleaded as respondents in Cont.Cas(C) 1058/2023** (contempt for violating DRT order in SBI vs PPJ) proves:
- They dealt with PPJ Corpus assets
- The DRT/SBI proceedings treated them as connected to PPJ
- Bills dated 20.08.2023 were issued by R-2 (Rashi Khemka) and R-3 (Pawan Gupta) — showing they operated PPJ business

#### (iv) Pattern of Asset Dissipation Through These Entities:
- PPJ Corpus assets are being dissipated through multiple family-controlled companies
- Without joining these entities, any partition decree would be rendered hollow — Defendants could simply transfer assets to these entities to defeat the decree
- The contempt proceedings (violation of restraining order) demonstrate this pattern was already recognized by the DRT
